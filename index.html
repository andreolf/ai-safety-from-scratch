About

AI Safety from Scratch is a personal, free project by Francesco Andreoli. Francesco works at MetaMask (Consensys) as Head of Developer Relations and works on agent wallets. This site is not an official MetaMask or Consensys product and is not endorsed by them, or by ETH Zurich, OWASP, any AI company or any government body. The agents-and-payments lessons (Phase 06) are written to be wallet-neutral. Where MetaMask is mentioned, it is one example among others, and the page says so.

Certificates are free certificates of completion, not accredited.

No accounts, no tracking, no paywall. Progress stays in your browser.

Every fact links to its source; sources were last checked on 26 October 2026 and are re-checked every quarter.
