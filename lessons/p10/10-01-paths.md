You have reached the last foundations phase. Maybe AI safety is now a hobby you want to keep up. Maybe you are wondering whether it could be a job. Either way, the first surprise is how many different kinds of work it takes.

AI safety is not only for machine-learning researchers. The career advice organisation 80,000 Hours lists AI-focused paths that include technical research, governance and policy, security, field-building and advocacy. You can start from the skills you already have.

Five AI-focused career paths listed by 80,000 Hours, and the kind of work in each.
:::end

## The paths, in plain words

80,000 Hours is a nonprofit that researches high-impact careers. Its list of AI-focused career paths includes “AI governance and policy”, “AI safety technical research”, “AI safety fieldbuilding”, “AI security”, “AI safety advocacy” and others. Its short descriptions:

:::source url="https://80000hours.org/career-reviews/" by="80,000 Hours, Career reviews" date="" checked="26 Oct 2026"
Technical governance: Bridge technical expertise and policy impact. AI security: Harden systems and prevent model theft. AI safety technical research: Develop techniques to make AI safe. AI safety advocacy: Get decision-makers to act on AI risk.
:::end

What that looks like day to day:

- **Technical research:** the work of Phases 07 and 08: alignment, interpretability, evals. Usually needs strong maths or programming.
- **Governance and policy:** the work of Phase 09: writing, analysing and enforcing rules. Lawyers, economists and civil servants bring useful skills.
- **Security:** protecting AI systems and the data around them, including the attacks of Phases 04 to 06. Software and security engineers fit here, and the optional Builder track (Phase 11) is a taste of it.
- **Field-building and communication:** courses, events, journalism and explainers that help others understand the field. This course is a small example.
- **Operations:** every research group and institute needs people who run finance, hiring and events.

## Where to get advice

80,000 Hours offers free one-to-one advising calls and a free AI career advisor chatbot on its site. Its problem profile on artificial intelligence explains why it ranks AI among the most pressing problems. You do not have to agree with all of its views to use its career guides.

:::intern proud
The intern thought AI safety meant a PhD in maths, so it was not for them. Then they noticed that this course needed someone to check sources, write plain English and test labs on a phone. That is field-building, and they had been doing it all along.
---
Start from what you are already good at. Most paths into AI safety need some people who are not researchers.
:::end

:::exercise Match your skills
Write down two things you are good at. For each one, pick the path from the list above where it would be most useful, and one small step you could take this month.
:::solution
Example: “I explain things clearly” fits field-building and communication: write a short explainer for friends about voice-clone scams (Phase 03). “I work in procurement” fits governance and policy: read the AI Act’s high-risk list (lesson 09.02) and check whether any tool your employer buys is on it. There is no wrong answer; the point is to see that your skills already have a place.
:::end
