## The hook

You have learned a lot in ten phases. In a month, what will you actually do differently? Most good intentions fade unless they are written down, specific and somewhere you will see them.

## The one idea

**Pick a few specific habits, write them on one page, and review the page every three months. A short plan you follow beats a long one you forget.**

:::lab plan-builder

## What goes on the plan

The lab offers four groups of habits, each from earlier phases:

- **When AI answers me:** check important facts in a second source, open the sources yourself, treat a confident tone as no evidence, and notice when a chatbot agrees with everything (Phase 02).
- **Scams and my family:** agree a family code word, call back on a number you already know, and never pay because of a call, a video or an ad alone (Phase 03).
- **Agents that act for me:** fewest tools and permissions, ask before acting, caps and end dates, and never hand over keys (Phases 04 to 06).
- **Keep learning:** one evidence source a month, one free course, and teaching one idea to someone else (lessons 10.02 and 10.03).

The FBI’s advice on AI-powered fraud backs the family habits:

:::source url="https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2024/PSA241203" by="FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center, Alert I-120324-PSA" date="3 Dec 2024" checked="26 Oct 2026"
Create a secret word or phrase with your family to verify their identity.
:::end

The same alert suggests that, if possible, you “limit online content of your image or voice, make social media accounts private, and limit followers to people you know”.

## Make it a family plan

Your plan works better if the people around you share it. Sit down with the people you would call in an emergency, agree the code word and show them the plan. Security from Scratch, our sister course, is preparing a family lesson called “The 30-minute safety afternoon” (linked below once it is published). Never write the code word itself on the plan: agree it face to face.

## Keep it alive

- Put the printed page where you will see it, for example next to the computer.
- Put a reminder in your calendar to review it every three months.
- When something changes, such as a new agent or a new bank app, add one line.

:::intern proud
The intern’s first plan said only “be careful”. The new one has eight habits, a calendar reminder and a code word agreed with their grandmother over coffee. When a “bank” calls the following week, the intern hangs up and calls back on the number on their card.
---
Specific beats vague. “Call back on a number I already know” is a plan. “Be careful” is a wish.
:::end

:::exercise Make one habit specific
Take the vague goal “be careful with AI agents” and rewrite it as two specific habits someone could check.
:::solution
For example: “Every agent that can pay has a weekly cap of 50 francs and permission that ends on Friday” and “The agent must ask me before it sends any email to someone new.” Each one is specific enough that you can check whether you did it.
:::end
